Popular FAQs

How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery takes 2 to 3 business days within Colombo and suburbs, and 3 to 5 business days for outstation areas.
What is the return policy?
We accept returns on all unused products in their original packaging within 30 days of purchase. Warranty claims are processed separately.
Can I modify my delivery address?
Addresses can be modified within 2 hours of placing an order. Please use our Support Chat to speak to an agent immediately for updates.
