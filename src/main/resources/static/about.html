Our Story

Ceylon Letter Co started with a passion for creating minimal, premium, and long-lasting jewellery in Sri Lanka.

We focus on chains, bracelets, rings, earrings, and custom-designed pendants made with stainless steel and plated materials.

Our goal is simple:

Good quality. Clean design. Affordable luxury.

We aim to bring international-level jewellery quality to the local market.
