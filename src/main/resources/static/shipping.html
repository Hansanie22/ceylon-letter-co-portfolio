Customer Support Response Time

  • Our customer support team responds within 24 hours on working days.

  • After placing an order, customers may be contacted via phone or WhatsApp for confirmation if needed.

International Customs & Duties

  • For international orders, customers are responsible for any customs duties, taxes, or import charges imposed by their country.
  • Delivery delays may occur due to customs clearance processes, which are outside our control.

Lost or Delayed Shipments

  • If your parcel is delayed or not received within the expected delivery time, please contact our support team.
  • We will coordinate with the courier service to investigate the issue.
  • Resolution time depends on courier investigation results.
